What the colours mean

  • 7Dark digit. A clue from the puzzle. It cannot be changed.
  • 7Blue digit. Yours.
  • 7Green digit. Placed by Hint. Always correct.
  • 7Red on red. Your digit repeats one in its row, column or box, or Check (or Show mistakes) found it wrong. Clues and hints are never marked, so the red one is the one to fix.
  • Light tint. The row, column and box of the selected cell. Switch it off in Options.
  • Grey. With Cell assist on: every cell where the selected digit can no longer go, because its row, column or box already has one.
  • 7Stronger tint. Cells holding the same digit as the selected one.
  • 1357Highlighted pencil mark. Matches the selected digit.
  • Green flash. A row, column or box just filled with no repeats. It only checks the rules, not the solution.
Keyboard: 1–9 place, ⌫ erase, N pencil, arrows move, Ctrl/⌘+Z undo, Esc close this.
